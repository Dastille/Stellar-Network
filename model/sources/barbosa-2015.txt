The energy use related to supplemental artificial lighting was determined by assuming a 24-h photoperiod and a recommended daily Photosynthetically Active Radiation (PAR) of 17 mol/m2/day of both natural and supplemental light [5], for optimal lettuce production. It was also assumed that half of the required radiation could be obtained from natural lighting.
